The Business

What We Do

We bridge the gap between visionary founders and institutional capital. Terra Firma Global identifies, develops, and facilitates transformative real estate and infrastructure projects that integrate regenerative and sustainable practices. Our advisory and fundraising services help founders, operators, and sponsors access capital, strategic expertise, and market insights to scale impact-driven businesses.

Our Thesis

Real estate and infrastructure represent the largest asset classes globally and are fundamental to climate solutions, community resilience, and biodiversity restoration. We believe regenerative development models—integrating ecological restoration, social equity, and financial returns—are becoming the baseline for institutional capital allocation. Founders and operators who embed regenerative principles into their business models unlock differentiated market positioning, attract mission-aligned capital, and create measurable impact alongside superior risk-adjusted returns.

How We Help

Through capital introductions, financial structuring, business planning, and advisory services, we help founders and operators navigate fundraising, market entry, and growth. We work across transaction facilitation, direct investment advisory, and strategic consulting to connect visionary capital with transformative real estate, sustainable hospitality, mass timber, and climate-aligned infrastructure projects.
